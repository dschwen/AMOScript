Original AMCAF examples

1/12 original programs compile. The quick-maths example runs with mouse input, fullscreen and Canvas/WebGL. The remaining programs have explicit blocker pages; they are not presented as working demos. All sources and four supplied data files are preserved under examples/AMCAF. Builds use AMCAF 1.50beta4 in extension slot 8.

Try the working example

Quick sine, cosine, angle and square root: move the pointer to aim the line and resize the circle. Press Escape outside fullscreen or a mouse button to exit the AMOS program.
