Screen Open 0,320,200,16,Lowres
'No multitasking...  
 Multi No 
'DOUBLE BUFFER...THANX !!! 
Double Buffer 
'WAIT UNTIL SCREENS PROPERLY INITIALISED...  
Wait Vbl 
'CANCEL THE DOUBLE BUFFERING PROCESS...  
Autoback 0
'Ok, define the first BLIT.  
 Blit Store Left 0,1,0,0 To 320,120,-1
'SWAP THE SCREENS AND THEREFORE ALSO THE BITMAPS ETC...  
Screen Swap 
'Define the second BLIT... 
 Blit Store Left 0,2,0,0 To 320,120,-1
'REACTIVATE THE DOUBLE BUFFERING PROCESS ! 
Autoback 2
'BRING YOUR STUFF ON SCREEN... 
For I=1 To 25
   Print "AMOS!AMOS!AMOS!AMOS!AMOS!AMOS!AMOS!AMOS"
Next 
Repeat 
    Vbl Wait 200
   'Ok, do the scrolling on the Physical and Logical screen...
    Multi Blit 1 To 2
Until Left Click 
  Blit Erase 1
  Blit Erase 2
'ENABLE MULTITASKING...
 Multi Yes
