 Multi No 
Sprite 2,X Hard(29),Y Hard(182),2
 Reserve Check 650
Reserve Zone 650
X=0
Y=0
_ZONE=1
' Install some zones...
For I=1 To 65
   For J=1 To 10
       Set Check _ZONE,X,Y To X+14,Y+4
      Set Zone _ZONE,X,Y To X+14,Y+4
      Add X,15
      Inc _ZONE
   Next 
   X=0
   Add Y,3
Next 
'Check zone's speed increase comes from the fact that you can  
'restrict the check to certain zones.
T=Timer
For I=1 To 1000
   _ZONE= Hit Spr Check(400 To 650,0,0,2)
   '  Checks from zone 400 to 650 
Next 
Print "Check Time in VBL ";Timer-T
T=Timer
For I=1 To 1000
   _ZONE=Hzone(X Sprite(2),Y Sprite(2))
   '  Checks from zone 1 to 650 
Next 
Print "Zone Time in VBL ";Timer-T
