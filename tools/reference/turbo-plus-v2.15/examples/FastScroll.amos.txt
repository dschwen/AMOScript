Screen Open 1,320,256,8,Lowres
'turn off multitasking for much better screen synch
 Multi No 
' FIRST lets scroll with normal AMOS instructions! 
SHIFT=1
BLITS=10
 Blit Clear -1
Y=0
For I=1 To BLITS
   '  USE A POSITIVE SHIFT FOR SHIFTING TO THE RIGHT !!!
   Def Scroll I,0,Y To 320,Y+20,-SHIFT,0
   Add Y,20
   Inc SHIFT
Next 
Locate 0,0
For I=1 To 25
   Print "jkdshfkjdsahflkjahdslkhagsdjhsjhhdhsksk"
Next 
Draw 0,200 To 300,200
For I=1 To 100
    Vbl Wait 255 : Rem we even let AMOS use Multi No & Vbl Wait, but still ugly 
   For J=1 To BLITS
      Scroll J
   Next 
Next 
Print "THAT WAS AMOS!  Not very pretty!"
Print "Left click for TURBO!"
Repeat : Until Left Click 

 Blit Clear -1
Locate 0,0
For I=1 To 25
   Print "jkdshfkjdsahflkjahdslkhagsdjhsjhhdhsksk"
Next 
SHIFT=1
BLITS=10
Y=0
'  DEFINE AND STORE ALL THE BLITS
For I=1 To BLITS
   '  USE A POSITIVE SHIFT FOR SHIFTING TO THE RIGHT !!!
    Blit Store Left 1,I,0,Y To 320,Y+20,-SHIFT
   Add Y,20
Next 
' THIS IS ONLY DONE TO SHOW THAT IT WORKS...I COULD HAVE CHANGED THE 
' SHIFT VALUE WHEN DEFINING THE BLITS... 
For I=1 To 10
    Blit Speed I,I
Next 
' OK...HERE COMES THE SPEED !!! JUST FEED THE BLITTER WITH THE PRE-
' CALCULATED VALUES !!!
For I=1 To 100
    Vbl Wait 255
    Multi Blit 1 To BLITS
Next 
' DON'T FORGET TO FREE THE MEMORY USED BY THE BLIT DEFINITIONS !!! 
For I=1 To BLITS
    Blit Erase I
Next 
 Multi Yes
